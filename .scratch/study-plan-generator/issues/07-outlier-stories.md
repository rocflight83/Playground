# 07: Outlier stories

**What to build:** At each phase boundary, the plan shows me a real person or case that reached this skill by an unusual route, and distils why the unusual approach worked into a principle I can transfer. Every story carries a working citation I can follow. Subject-specific examples are used where they exist; well-documented general ones where they do not.

The rule that matters most: no citation means no story. A phase with no citable example renders without one, with no placeholder and no invention. A fabricated example absorbed as fact is worse than a missing section.
